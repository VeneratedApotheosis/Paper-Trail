# PaperTrail — Demo Talking Script (~2.5 min)

> Target: 2–3 minutes spoken. ~420 words at a natural pace. Cues in **[brackets]** are screen actions, not spoken.
> Rule: everything shown is synthetic/seeded data, and the app never sends email or moves money.

---

## 0:00 — The hook (15s)
Your inbox already knows what stores owe you. You just never get around to reading it: the refund that never showed up, the return window closing this week, the price that dropped three days after you paid.

This is **PaperTrail**. It reads your gmail and scans every purchase, then finds the money you're actually owed.

## 0:15 — Connect & trust (20s)
**[Connect screen]**
When you connect to PaperTrail, we ask for one scope: `gmail.readonly`. That's it. Before Google even loads, we show you exactly what we read, what we store, and what we will *never* do — we never send email, we never move money, and we redact card numbers, phones, and addresses before anything leaves your device.

**[Receipts stream into the timeline]**
Watch the ledger fill in. Each email becomes a clean row — merchant, order number, date, line items, total — and every field links back to a quote from the source email with a confidence score. Five emails about one order? We merge them into a single timeline: ordered, shipped, returned, refunded. Anything the AI isn't sure about goes to a **Needs review** queue and stays out of your totals until you confirm it.

**[Receipts stream into the timeline]**

**[Click "Use demo inbox"]**
For this demo I'll use a seeded, synthetic inbox — same pipeline as a real account.

## 1:05 — The hero number (35s)
**[Money Ledger hero: "$187 waiting for you"]**
And here's the whole point — one number. **You're owed over a thousand dollars.**

It breaks down into three findings. Refunds that have been stuck and overdue. Return windows closing in less than a week. And  price drops on something you've already bought.

**[Open a "Why?" panel]**
Every single finding has a "Why?" — the rule that detected the savings, the dates, and the exact email quote behind it. The numbers come from deterministic rules, never from the AI. The AI only reads and writes drafts.

## 1:40 — One-tap claim (20s)
**[Claim draft sheet]**
Tap a finding and we draft the claim for you — citing the order, the amount, and the policy quote, written in your name. You review it, edit it, and approve. Then it's copy-to-clipboard or a `mailto:` — *you* send it. PaperTrail never sends anything for you.

## 2:00 — Trust & delete (20s)
**[Trust page → Delete everything]**
Finally, the Trust page: a full audit log of every fetch, every AI call, every approval. And one button — **Delete everything** — revokes the token, purges every row, and empties the ledger live.

## 2:20 — Close (10s)
PaperTrail: your inbox already has the receipts. We just read the paper trail — and hand you back the money you're owed.
